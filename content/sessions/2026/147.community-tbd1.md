As models from both frontier and OSS labs have become more capable, Airflow use cases have expanded beyond traditional deterministic workflows, raising new questions about how to orchestrate probabilistic processes safely and reliably.

In this roundtable, participants will exchange and their perspectives and experiences with authoring, managing, monitoring, and evaluating agentic systems and workflows in and around Apache Airflow.
