Yossi is a Principal Engineer at Incredibuild and founding technical member of islo.dev. He builds persistent Linux sandboxes for AI agents and unified cache layers for massive codebases and workflows. His talks focus on the unglamorous plumbing of Software 3.0: caches, compile farms, and the stateful infrastructure needed to make AI coding scale.
