Saket Milind Karve is a Software Engineer specializing in Robotics and ML infrastructure, with 6+ years bridging research and production engineering for foundation models. At Dyna Robotics, he builds Airflow pipelines processing 3,000 video hours per hour for VLA and World Action Model training. Previously at Amazon Lab126, he led a 5-person team building robot memory systems. He holds an MS in Computer and Information Science from UPenn.
