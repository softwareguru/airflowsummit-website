Cesar is a data engineering and AI specialist who designs agentic AI architectures and develops enterprise semantic layers. He has experience across smart cities, pharma, fintech and automotive. He is a member of the Anthropic Builders Council, Claude Ambassador for Mexico City and founder of the AWS AI User Group Mexico. He has been building communities and advocating for open science since 2017.
