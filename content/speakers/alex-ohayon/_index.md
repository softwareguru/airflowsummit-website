After studying Software Engineering both in France at Mines Paristech and in the US at MIT, Alex started his career at Google Tel Aviv in 2015 building the first ML models for Waze. 12 years later, he leads all Data, ML and AI infrastructures allowing Waze to scale with its 200+ million monthly active users.
