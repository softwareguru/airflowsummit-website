Ashwin Sampathkumar is an Engineering Manager at Google, specializing in AI/ML and workflow automation. Throughout his 18-year career, including leadership tenures at Salesforce and Signet Jewelers, he has focused on building data-driven systems and predictive ML models at scale. He is a recipient of the Google Core Tech Award in 2024 and the Motorola Scholar (FAER) Award in 2006. His expertise lies in agentic automation, event-driven SOA architecture, and building next generational solutions.
