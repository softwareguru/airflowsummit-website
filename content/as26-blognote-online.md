### Austin was just the beginning. On November 4 - 5, the Airflow community reconnects online, with free access, new sessions, and selected highlights from the in-person Summit.

Austin was unforgettable: three days of keynotes, hallway conversations, and the kind of ideas that only happen when the Apache Airflow® community is in the same room. But not everyone could travel, and great talks always outnumber the slots on the schedule.

That's why we're bringing back **Airflow Summit Online Reconnect**, happening **November 4 & 5, 2026**, fully **online and free** to attend.

<br>

### What to expect

Online Reconnect isn't a replay of Austin — it's an extension of it. Over two days you'll find:

* **New sessions** from speakers who couldn't travel to the in-person Summit, presented live.

* **Selected highlights** from Airflow Summit 2026, for anyone who missed them or wants a second look.

* **A virtual expo** hall where you can connect directly with Google Cloud, Astronomer, and BMC.

* **Accessible recordings**, access to the Slack community, and virtual swag.

More than 3,000 people joined us online last time, from every time zone. The format is built around that: global focus, no travel, no ticket.

<br>

### Want to speak?

The Call for Proposals is open. If you have a story about how your team runs Airflow — a migration, a scaling problem, a pattern worth sharing — this is your chance to tell it to a worldwide audience from wherever you are.
